# Limitations and deviations

- Empirical coverage means do not prove finite-sample validity; Claims 4 and 5
  provide the mechanism certificates.
- CA uses nominal 0.95 and CCP uses nominal 0.90, so their coverage summaries
  are not pooled.
- The paper/released-code F1/F2/F3 mismatch is disclosed and source-hash-bound.
- One CA finite-seed length-SD scalar differs from the table (`0.201899`
  observed versus `0.18` reported); its companion mean and coverage scalars
  agree and the efficiency conclusion is unchanged.
