# Limitations and deviations

- Empirical coverage is not claimed to equal exactly 0.9.
- The minimum released mean is 0.890299; although within the predeclared
  reproduction tolerance, it warrants explicit reviewer attention.
- Exact dependence enumerations are mechanism checks, not the basis of the
  universal theorem verdict.
- The immutable released full protocol is independently re-aggregated rather
  than retrained; every one of its 11,700 raw cells is checked.
