# Limitations and deviations

- The falsification is literal and endpoint-specific. It does not undermine
  the corrected uniqueness result on positive p-values.
- The counterexample produces exactly the same e-values as AoN on all
  conformal p-values; it has no empirical performance difference.
- Exact finite rank sweeps are not presented as proof of a universal theorem.
  The measure-zero endpoint argument is the complete counterexample.
- No paper algorithm or dataset is substituted. This is a theorem claim and
  is evaluated using a proof-level certificate rather than a proxy experiment.
